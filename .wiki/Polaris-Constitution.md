*The design language itself: what Polaris is, and where the canonical text lives.*

**Polaris** — is the constitutional layer of this repository: the worldview, values, principles, and engineering laws from which everything else (including Axis, the operating framework this wiki mostly describes) is derived.

> Software is the intentional organisation of human knowledge into executable systems that transform reality in service of humanity.

## Where the truth lives

The canonical, ratified text lives **in the repository**, not in this wiki: [`polaris/`](https://github.com/pedrobritx/polaris/tree/refactor/polaris-capability-extraction/polaris). This page is a map, and it must never contradict the canon.

## Polaris vs Axis

Polaris states *why* and *what must hold*; Axis (maintained in its own engineering repository) encodes *how a project operates day to day*. When they conflict, the constitution wins, and the conflict is resolved through an ADR — see [ADR-001](https://github.com/pedrobritx/polaris/blob/refactor/polaris-capability-extraction/decisions/adr/001-polaris-canon-three-surfaces.md) for how the repo, this wiki, and the future website relate.
