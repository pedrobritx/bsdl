*The front door to Polaris governance and its independent capabilities.*

> **The repository is the company. Humans and AI are just different kinds of employees.**

Polaris holds the Constitution, governance and orchestration registry. Axis supplies independently maintained engineering roles, templates and playbooks; Meridian supplies design practice; Sentinel supplies security guidance. Their composition preserves context and decisions across sessions without compulsory shared runtime dependencies.

**Who is this for?** Builders who work with AI development tools (Claude Code, Codex, Gemini CLI, Cursor, Amp — Axis is model-agnostic) and are tired of re-explaining their project every session, relearning old mistakes, and losing the "why" behind decisions. If you want every project to start from established standards and every decision to be traceable, this is your front door.
